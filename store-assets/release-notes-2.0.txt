Version 2.0 — the biggest update yet:

• Where to stream it — every movie, show, and anime now displays streaming service logos for your country (data by JustWatch), with direct Crunchyroll/Netflix links on anime
• Exact episode info — TV cards show which episode airs that day (S19 E7); anime cards add airing countdowns and synopses
• Search any day — filter the selected date's releases as you type, original-language titles included
• Now in 6 languages — English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Nederlands
• Light & dark themes that follow your system setting, with accessible contrast in both
• Much faster — the popup paints instantly from cache while fresh data loads, uses fewer network requests, and weighs about 30 KB
• Fixed: dates now resolve in your local timezone (evening browsing no longer showed the previous day's releases in some regions)

As always: no accounts, no analytics, no data collection — release data comes from TMDB and AniList, and nothing leaves your browser.
